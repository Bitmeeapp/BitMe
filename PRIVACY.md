# Privacy Policy for Bitme

**Effective Date:** October 8, 2026

**Bitme** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our offline peer-to-peer messaging application handles your information.

---

### 1. Zero Personal Data Collection
Bitme is a 100% offline peer-to-peer (P2P) messaging application. 
* We **do not** collect, store, track, or share any personal information.
* We **do not** require account creation, phone numbers, email addresses, or social media logins.
* We **do not** run any central servers, analytics tools, or tracking code.

---

### 2. Local Device Permissions
To enable offline device discovery and message transmission, Bitme requests access to specific hardware features on your Android device:

* **Bluetooth & Nearby Devices (`BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`, `BLUETOOTH_SCAN`, `BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE`, `NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES`):** Used strictly to discover and establish direct P2P connections with nearby devices running Bitme.
* **Location Permission (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` / `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`):** Required by the Android OS to scan for nearby Bluetooth and Wi-Fi devices. Bitme **does not** track, store, or transmit your physical GPS location.

---

### 3. Data Storage and Messaging Security
* **Direct Transmission:** Messages are transmitted directly from one device to another via Bluetooth or Local Wi-Fi (UDP broadcast). 
* **No Server Storage:** Messages never pass through or get saved on any external servers.
* **Local Storage:** Your chosen display name and chat histories are stored locally on your device only. You can clear this data anytime by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
Bitme does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services
Bitme does not use any third-party SDKs, ad networks, or cloud services.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this policy if app features change. Any updates will be posted on this page.
